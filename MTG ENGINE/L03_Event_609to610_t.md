# 1. EFFECTS — WHAT THEY ARE (Rule 609)

An effect is anything that happens in the game as a result of a spell or ability. Effects are the outputs of the engine — what actually changes the game state after a spell resolves, an ability activates, or a triggered ability fires.

## 1.1 Types of Effects

| Type | Duration | Example |
|---|---|---|
| One-shot effect | Instantaneous | "Destroy target creature" |
| Continuous effect | Duration-based | "Creatures get +1/+1 until end of turn" |
| Replacement effect | Modifies events | "If a creature would die, exile it instead" |
| Prevention effect | Blocks damage | "Prevent the next 3 damage" |
| Delayed triggered ability | Future trigger | "At the beginning of your next upkeep, draw a card" |

## 1.2 Effects from Static Abilities

Static abilities generate **continuous effects** while the ability exists and its conditions are met (rule 604, 611.2). These are not one-shot effects — they don't happen once and end; they are ongoing states.

## 1.3 Effects Happen During Resolution

Effects of a spell or ability happen when that spell or ability resolves (rule 608). For triggered abilities, effects happen when the trigger resolves. Effects do not happen when something is cast or when a triggered ability is put on the stack.

# 2. ONE-SHOT EFFECTS (Rule 610)

A one-shot effect happens once and is complete. "Draw a card," "destroy target permanent," "deal 3 damage" — these execute and are done. There is no ongoing component.

## 2.1 One-Shot Effects and Timing

One-shot effects execute as part of resolution. During resolution of a spell or ability, no player has priority and no one can respond to individual instructions within the resolution. The entire effect executes, then priority is returned.

## 2.2 Delayed Triggered Abilities (610.3)

Some one-shot effects create **delayed triggered abilities** — they set up a future trigger condition at the time of resolution, but the effect itself doesn't happen until that trigger fires.

"At the beginning of your next upkeep, draw a card" — when the spell resolves, the one-shot effect is creating a delayed trigger. The draw itself happens later.

**Key rule:** A delayed triggered ability created by a resolving effect uses the characteristics of objects (power, toughness, types, etc.) at the time the trigger **fires**, not at the time the delayed trigger was created — unless the ability specifies otherwise.

**Exception:** If the delayed trigger says "that creature" or refers to a specific object, it tracks the object. If the object is no longer in the relevant zone when the trigger fires, the trigger either does nothing (if the object must be present) or checks last known information (if relevant).

## 2.3 One-Shot Effects That Modify Objects

Some one-shot effects temporarily modify objects ("until end of turn"). These appear to be one-shot but they actually create **continuous effects with a time-limited duration** (rule 611.3). "Creatures get +1/+1 until end of turn" is a continuous effect, not a one-shot — it creates an effect that applies continuously until the cleanup step.

The distinction matters because:
- A one-shot effect that says "put a +1/+1 counter" creates a permanent counter (zone changes remove it, but the counter itself is persistent while the creature is on the battlefield)
- A continuous effect that says "+1/+1 until end of turn" applies at layer 7c, ends in cleanup, and is not a counter

## 2.4 Multiple Effects from One Spell/Ability

A spell or ability may contain multiple effects. They are executed in the order listed in the text. If any effect is impossible, that instruction is skipped (101.3) and subsequent effects still execute.

---

# 3. CROSS-LAYER CONNECTIONS

**→ Layer 8 (Continuous Effects, 611–613):** Continuous effects generated by "until end of turn" wording are processed through the layer system. One-shot effects that create permanent changes (counters, zone changes) bypass the layer system — they directly alter game state.

**→ Layer 3 (Replacement, 614–616):** Replacement effects intercept events that would be caused by one-shot effects. "If [permanent] would be destroyed, exile it instead" replaces the one-shot destruction event with a different event.

**→ Layer 4 (Triggers, 603):** Delayed triggered abilities (610.3) are created by one-shot effects and then processed through the standard 603 trigger rules when they fire.
