# 1. THE MONARCH (Rule 724)

## 1.1 What the Monarch Is

The Monarch is a designation held by exactly one player. At the beginning of the Monarch's end step, they draw a card.

## 1.2 Becoming and Losing the Monarch

A player becomes the Monarch when an effect says they do. In Commander, any card that creates the Monarch mechanic makes the first player who achieves it the Monarch.

A player loses the Monarch status and another player becomes the Monarch when a creature that player controls deals combat damage to the current Monarch. The attacker's controller becomes the Monarch.

## 1.3 The Monarch in Commander

Only one player can be the Monarch at a time. When a creature deals combat damage to the Monarch, that creature's controller steals the Monarch status — even if multiple creatures attack the Monarch in the same combat, only the last one to deal damage matters (or the first if damage is assigned simultaneously — the ability triggers for each).

## 1.4 Engine Connections

- "Become the Monarch" is a triggered ability effect
- "At the beginning of the Monarch's end step" is a triggered ability that fires at the beginning of the end step of whoever is currently the Monarch
- The Monarch designation is tracked globally — it's not a counter or status on a card

# 2. THE INITIATIVE (Rule 725)

## 2.1 What the Initiative Is

The Initiative is a designation similar to the Monarch. The player who has the Initiative ventures into the Dungeon at the beginning of their upkeep — specifically into Undercity, a specific dungeon used by the Initiative mechanic.

## 2.2 Taking and Losing the Initiative

A player takes the Initiative when an effect says they do. Another player takes the Initiative when a creature they control deals combat damage to the current Initiative holder — same theft mechanic as the Monarch.

## 2.3 Undercity

The Undercity is a specific dungeon card that must be used with the Initiative. When a player takes the Initiative for the first time, they put Undercity into the command zone (if it isn't already) and venture into it. Subsequent Initiative upkeep triggers also venture into Undercity.

## 2.4 Engine Connections

- The Initiative venture trigger fires at the beginning of each upkeep of the Initiative holder
- Damage-theft is a triggered ability ("whenever a creature deals combat damage to [holder], [attacker's controller] takes the Initiative")
- The Initiative and Monarch can coexist — different players can hold each

# 3. RAD COUNTERS (Rule 727)

Rad counters are placed on players by specific card effects. At the beginning of a player's precombat main phase, if they have one or more rad counters:
1. They mill cards equal to their rad counter count
2. They remove a rad counter for each nonland card milled this way
3. They take damage equal to the number of nonland cards milled

This creates a recurring cost on the player while they hold rad counters. In Commander, rad counter accumulation can become a significant life loss engine.

## 3.1 Engine Connection

The rad counter trigger (122.1i) fires at the beginning of the precombat main phase. This is a triggered ability generated by the rad counter state — it routes through Layer 4 trigger insertion.

# 4. DAY AND NIGHT (Rule 730)

## 4.1 The Day/Night Cycle

Day and Night are global game states. At any moment, the game is either in Day, Night, or neither (before the mechanic is active).

**Day becomes Night** at the beginning of an end step if the active player cast no spells that turn.
**Night becomes Day** at the beginning of an end step if at least two spells were cast that turn by any player.

## 4.2 Triggering Day/Night Tracking

Day/Night only begins to be tracked once any card that references Day or Night is in play. Before that, the game is in neither state.

## 4.3 Daybound and Nightbound (702.145)

Permanents with Daybound transform to their back face when Night begins. Permanents with Nightbound transform to their front face when Day begins. These transformations happen as a triggered ability at the beginning of the end step when the state changes.

## 4.4 Engine Connections

- Day/Night state changes are triggered by state-based conditions checked at the end step
- Daybound/Nightbound transformations are triggered abilities (702.145)
- Day/Night tracks from the entire game's perspective — all players can contribute spells toward the "two spells" threshold for Night→Day
- In Commander with four players, the increased spell count per turn makes Day→Night less common and Night→Day more common
