# 1. ATTRACTION CARDS (Rule 717)

Attraction cards are used only in Unfinity-specific gameplay and are not relevant to standard Commander. They use an attraction deck separate from the main library and are "opened" rather than drawn.

---

# 2. PROTOTYPE CARDS (Rule 718)

## 2.1 What Prototype Does

Prototype is an alternative casting cost that reduces the mana cost and power/toughness of the spell. A prototype card has two sets of P/T and costs: the printed main cost/stats and the prototype cost/stats.

## 2.2 Which Characteristics Apply

When cast normally: main cost and main P/T.
When cast for prototype cost: prototype cost and prototype P/T.

Once on the battlefield, the card has whichever characteristics correspond to how it was cast — until it leaves the battlefield.

## 2.3 Commander Relevance

Prototype cards are legal in Commander if their color identity fits. The prototype cost mana symbols also count toward color identity.

---

# 3. CASE CARDS (Rule 719)

Cases are enchantments with a "To Solve" condition. When the condition is met, the case becomes "Solved" and gains a different ongoing effect. Solving a case is a state-based action — when the "To Solve" condition is continuously met at an SBA check, the case becomes solved.

---

# 4. OMEN CARDS (Rule 720)

Omens are enchantments that can be cast for their main effect or used for their "Omen" ability — an activated ability that exiles the Omen from the battlefield to create a triggered effect. The Omen ability is an activated ability that sacrifices or exiles the enchantment as part of its cost or effect.

---

# 5. STATION CARDS (Rule 721)

Station cards are part of a linked pair mechanic. Each Station has a locked and unlocked state. Paying the unlock cost (a special action — 116.2m) advances the Station to its unlocked designation, enabling its abilities. Station pairs work together — one Station's unlocked state enables the other's abilities.

---

# 6. NOTE ON RARITY IN COMMANDER

Most of these card types (Attraction, some Prototype, Station) are format-specific or set-specific. In regular Commander play, the most relevant from this group are:
- **Prototype** — appears in The Brothers' War and related sets; legal in Commander
- **Case** — appears in Murders at Karlov Manor; legal in Commander
- **Omen** — appears in Tarkir: Dragonstorm and later; legal in Commander
