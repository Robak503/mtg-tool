## 1. Methodology — how residue was extracted

`classifyCard` does not expose the parked clause, so the census reuses its machinery per-line:
for each of the **17,395** body-only names (dedup law identical to `tier-fingerprint.mjs`), the
oracle was reminder-stripped, split into lines, and each line re-classified as a synthetic
one-line card `{name, type, mana, oracle: line}`. A line that classifies native is **modeled**;
a line that doesn't is **residue** — the clause that parks the card. Corrections applied:

- **Equipment context:** the equipped-bonus line and the Equip line only model as a pair, so
  Equip/Fortify lines ride along on every synthetic line (kills the false "equipped creature
  gets +N/+N" residue; what remains for Equipment is real — Equip—Pay-life variants, quoted
  grants, equipped-dies triggers).
- **Structural parks first:** aura / saga / adventure-half / DFC-pw-back / DFC-faces (`//` in
  oracle) / battle park as whole cards on a shape gate, not a clause — counted separately.
- **Modal blocks:** a "choose one —" header + its `•` bullets counted as ONE residue unit.
- **Whole-card law:** a card counts as a FLIP for family F iff **every** residue line belongs
  to F. `touched` = cards where F appears at all (partial-progress pool).

cards prove the difference); family "flips" are whole-card-law counts and assume the family's

first. All counts are distinct card names, native-wins dedup, printings collapsed.*
