LEYLINE · the frost

The glass recipe — six layers

Live specimen

This panel is the full stack: frost, lit top edge, diagonal sheen, and grain, floating on the aurora ground behind it.

L0
Aurora ground. Mint/teal radial orbs on near-black — the light the blur feeds on. radial-gradient(...rgba(45,212,191,.13)...) on #010202
L1
Frost. Translucent green-tinted fill + heavy blur with saturation lift. backdrop-filter: blur(18px) saturate(150%)
L2
Border. One mint hairline, alpha .16 — precise, never neon. border: 1px solid rgba(180,240,210,.16)
L3
Lit top edge. A 1px gradient line fading at both ends — the light source is above. ::before layer 1
L4
Sheen. Diagonal specular streak, gone by 42%. ::before layer 2, 118deg
L5
Grain. SVG turbulence at 5% kills banding, adds material. ::after, feTurbulence data-URI

Fallback: @supports not (backdrop-filter…) swaps the frost for solid rgba(13,28,20,.94). Blur is expensive — glass is for panels, never for every row; and the blur radius itself is never animated.
